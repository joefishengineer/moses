# Quality standards

These two checklists define what a good Moses answer and a good Moses lesson look like. Use them to check Moses's output yourself, to review pull requests that change INSTRUCTIONS.md, or as a quality bar if you build your own Moses.

- [Biblical research answers](biblical-research.md) covers any research answer: Nicene consistency, real citations, translation labels, mainstream scholarship, fringe views, Greek and Hebrew, and background.
- [Lessons, handouts, and series](lesson-planning.md) covers teaching material. It includes everything in the research standard, plus the teaching items.

Both standards follow the rules in [INSTRUCTIONS.md](../INSTRUCTIONS.md). If the two ever disagree, INSTRUCTIONS.md wins, so please open a pull request to bring the standard back in line.
